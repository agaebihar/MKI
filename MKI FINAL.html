    About this Dashboard

    Prepared from the Monthly Civil Accounts of the Government of Bihar compiled by the Office of the Accountant General (A&E), Bihar. All figures are progressive totals up to the month selected and are stated in ₹ Crore.

    Deficit indicators are computed on the Consolidated Fund transactions; ratios to GSDP use the GSDP figure adopted for the respective financial year.
